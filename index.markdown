[BrickCoLab](https://brickcolab.com) is a collection of services focused on using LEGO&reg; bricks for ice breakers, building community, communication skills, and critical thinking.

## BrickCoLab Classroom Activities

We offer these activities as a service to be integrated into your classroom. Work with us to schedule a date and choose an activity that best suits your needs.

### Ice Breakers

We offer a series of team-building activities that work well as ice breakers or early-semester engagement.

#### Building Competitions
Students are placed in groups, given a bin of LEGO&reg; bricks, and a task. Results are showcased and voted on to choose a winning group. Previous challenges include tallest freestanding structure and best art piece.

#### Blind Build
A great communication-building activity. Students are placed in groups: one part receives instructions for a small LEGO&reg; kit, while the other receives the bricks. Using different communication methods, they must work together to complete the build. Results can be quite funny.

> Warning: competitions can get quite heated!

### Hackathons

Hackathons are best run over a 2–5 hour session (or full day). Topics can be customized to align with your course content.

#### Build a Solution
We can design a custom hackathon where students are given an objective and must construct a solution using LEGO&reg; bricks. Examples include building a library of the future, designing a control panel, or creating solutions related to poverty.

#### Build & Code Race
Students are given a remote-controlled LEGO&reg; car, an Xbox controller, and part of a racetrack. They can customize both the car and track using LEGO&reg; bricks and optimize the code. The activity ends with races.

#### Introduction to Code
Want to introduce students to coding? We run a one-day session where students learn using [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/) or [Python](https://www.python.org/), building simple LEGO&reg; devices like a parking gate, remote-controlled car, or interactive puzzle.

### Brain Teasers

We offer a series of 15-minute activities that are perfect to start a class:

- Cube Puzzle  
- Tower Puzzle  
- Tetris Puzzle  
- Build a Duck  

### LEGO&reg; Escape Room

We offer a LEGO&reg; escape room experience. Set on an alien planet, participants must use an Xbox controller to navigate a Mars rover back to the command centre before the planet explodes.
